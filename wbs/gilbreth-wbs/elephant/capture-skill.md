# capture skill

## Outcome

The `elephant:capture` skill: auto-invokes on note-worthy work (hard-won learnings, decisions, open threads, boundary checkpoints — all of them) and writes small, well-linked notes into the knowledge graph, so future sessions can recall them. Broad capture, backed by the availability check and the conventions rulebook.

## Success Criteria

- `skills/capture/SKILL.md` auto-invokes on note-worthy moments regardless of whether the user mentioned Tusk.
- Notes written are small, linked via `[[wikilinks]]`/edges, and discoverable by indexation.
- Gates on the availability check (offer-bootstrap-once / dormant / silent).
